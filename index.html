About Me

I am Tran Ngoc Anh, a student of Class 11 Chemistry 1 at Hanoi – Amsterdam High School for the Gifted. I won Third Prize at the Vietnam National Excellent Student Contest in Chemistry (2025–2026) while still in grade 11, along with Second Prize at the Hanoi City Grade-12 Contest and two district First Prizes in middle school. My SAT score is 1520/1600 (Math 790). Beyond Chemistry, I play the piano (Silver Award at the ZhongSin International Music Competition 2024) and volunteer in the community, visiting and giving gifts to children with disabilities at the Thanh Xuan Peace Village.
